    Membership terms
    
 
    Fees
    
 
    Prices shown are the full amount for the period stated, payable at the start. There is no joining fee, locker fee or registration charge. Prices are a limited-period offer and may change for new members; your rate is fixed for the term you paid for.
    
 
    Duration and pauses
    
 
    A membership runs from the day you start. Freezes for medical reasons or long travel are handled case by case — talk to Dinesh before the fact, not after.
    
 
    Refunds
    
 
    Paid memberships are not refundable once the term begins. If the gym closes for reasons on our side, the missed days are added to your term.
    
 
    On the floor
    
 
    Re-rack your weights. Use a collar on loaded bars. Tell the coach about injuries, medication or heart conditions before you start. Training is at your own risk and you should get medical clearance if you're unsure.
    
 
    Hours
    
 
    Monday to Saturday 5:00am–10:00pm, Sunday 5:00pm–10:00pm. Public holidays may differ; we post changes on WhatsApp.
